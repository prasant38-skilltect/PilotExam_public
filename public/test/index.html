Atmosphere Composition

  • Nitrogen 78.08%
  • Oxygen 20.95%
  • N₂ + O₂ 99.03%
  • Argon 0.93%
  • Trace gases 0.04%

Major Greenhouse Gases

  • Carbon Dioxide (CO₂) The most significant greenhouse gas from human activities, primarily from burning fossil fuels.
  • Methane (CH₄) About 25 times more potent than CO₂, emitted from agriculture, landfills, and energy production.
  • Nitrous Oxide (N₂O) Nearly 300 times more potent than CO₂, mainly from agriculture and fossil fuel combustion.
  • Ozone (O₃) A greenhouse gas in the troposphere, but protective in the stratosphere.
  • Water Vapor (H₂O) The most abundant greenhouse gas, amplifying warming caused by other gases.
  • Fluorinated Gases Including CFCs, extremely potent but less abundant greenhouse gases.

The Ozone Layer
Protects Earth from harmful UV radiation by absorbing it in the stratosphere.
